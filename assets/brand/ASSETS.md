# Brand assets

## Studio hero, September 2026

Created with the built-in image generation tool using the existing Nexo monogram as the identity reference. These are conceptual brand visualizations, not photographs of company premises or client work.

- `nexo-studio-light.png`: portrait-friendly source, complete metallic N sculpture on a bright neutral background. Used for mobile.
- `nexo-studio-wide.png`: 3:1 panoramic adaptation used for desktop.

Prompt brief: preserve the exact interlocking N silhouette in brushed aluminum and petrol/turquoise enamel; quiet, naturally lit precision-design studio; no people, medical equipment, fake interfaces, neon, particles, text or extra objects; uninterrupted pale neutral negative space on the left for live HTML text. For the panoramic adaptation, place the complete sculpture on the right with ample margins above and below.

## Portfolio

`assets/portfolio/*-screen.png` are unretouched browser screenshots, captured by `scripts/capture-portfolio.cjs` from isolated browser contexts. They contain no private browser storage or client data. MedStock uses its bundled sample records.

Local versions: MedStock, Volia, Inner Oraculum. Public websites: Scriptorium and Psyche Lab. Older `*-hero` assets are illustrations and are no longer presented as application screenshots on the Nexo website.
